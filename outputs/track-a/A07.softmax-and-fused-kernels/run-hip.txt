Kernel 1 is SUPPOSED to fail: exp(100) = inf, so the normalizer is inf,
inf/inf = nan for the big score and finite/inf = 0 for every other one.

measured     : 0.036 ms unfused vs 0.014 ms fused -> 2.62x faster

The speedup normally lands BELOW the 3.5x traffic ratio: the scale and mask
kernels are pure streaming (they hit close to peak bandwidth) while the fused
kernel carries reduction barriers, and part of the unfused traffic may be
served by L2 rather than HBM. Compare the two numbers on your GPU and explain
the gap - that reasoning is the point of the lab.
