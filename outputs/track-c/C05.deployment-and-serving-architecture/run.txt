HOW TO READ THIS
====================================================================================================
  1. Static-at-peak is the honest upper bound on cost and lower bound on risk:
     $3456/day, 0.00% violations, 14% utilization. You are paying for the
     evening peak all night long.
  2. Reactive autoscaling saves $2506/day (73%) and buys +0.79 pp of SLO
     violations. Whether that trade is acceptable is a product decision, not an
     infrastructure one -- take it to the person who owns the SLO.
  3. The warm pool is priced insurance. It shows up directly in GPU-hours and
     directly reduces p99 during the ramp and the flash spike. Present it that
     way to a budget owner rather than as a technical detail.
  4. Panel 2 is the strategic result: shrinking the cold start moves the whole
     frontier. Local NVMe weight cache, faster checkpoint formats, and keeping a
     warm image beat any amount of controller tuning.
  5. Panel 3: scale-to-zero costs $65/day less than a single always-on replica,
     and takes SLO violations from 0.00% to 52.83%. If your SLO is anywhere near
     the cold start, scale-to-zero is not a cost optimization -- it is an outage
     you scheduled in advance, once per idle gap.
