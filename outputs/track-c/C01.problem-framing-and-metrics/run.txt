ROC-AUC barely moves because it is a ratio of rates (TPR vs FPR) and both rates are
computed within a class. PR-AUC collapses because precision has the negative count in
its denominator. On rare-positive problems, report PR-AUC (and its lift over the
prevalence baseline); an ROC-AUC of 0.9 can coexist with a useless precision.
