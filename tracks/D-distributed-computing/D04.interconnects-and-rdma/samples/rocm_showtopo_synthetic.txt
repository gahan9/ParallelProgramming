SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE OF THE `rocm-smi --showtopo` OUTPUT FORMAT.
Hand-written to exercise tools/topology_report.py. NOT captured from any machine, and
not a description of any product. No hostname, user, serial or address appears here
because nothing here came from a real system. See samples/README.md.

The shape it illustrates: the same two-island node as the NVIDIA sample, expressed in
the vocabulary ROCm has for it -- a link TYPE per pair and nothing finer. The Numa Nodes
section at the bottom is what lets the report call the PCIE pairs cross-socket; delete
that section and re-run to see the tool fall back to the conservative pcie-host reading
and say in place that it did.
