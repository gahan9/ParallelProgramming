Recall

Retrieval practice on the curriculum's own material. Answer before you reveal — the guessing is what does the work, not the reading.

Why cards and not re-reading: Roediger & Karpicke found students who re-tested recalled 61% of a passage after a week, versus 40% for students who re-read it the same number of times — even though the re-readers felt more confident (Test-Enhanced Learning, Psychological Science, 2006). Confidence is not retention.

How the boxes work. Every card starts in Box 1. Mark it Solid and it moves up a box; Shaky and it stays; Missed and it drops straight back to Box 1. Cards are drawn from Box 1 most often and Box 3 least, so the deck concentrates on what you keep getting wrong. This is a three-box Leitner system — deliberately simpler than a full spaced-repetition scheduler like SM-2, because interval tuning is not the thing standing between you and knowing this material.

Every card cites a paper, a vendor document, or the module it came from. If a card and its source disagree, the source wins — tell us and we will fix the card.
