Illustrative Gaussian bins, not a live production metric. The divergence score is a standard Population Stability Index computed over ten bins; the 0.1 / 0.25 thresholds shown are a common industry rule of thumb for "watch this" and "act on this", not a number this curriculum measured. The distinction is the citable part — the glossary's drift entry: covariate shift moves P(x) while the model's mapping stays valid; label/concept shift moves the relationship itself, which retraining on old labels cannot fix.
