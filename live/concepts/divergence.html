These are counts, not a measurement. Both phases always run to completion whenever at least one lane needs them — that is what "the sum of both paths" means. Real hardware can also predict a branch and skip a phase entirely if it turns out uniform after all, which this model does not represent. A03's deep dive traces this on AMD's 64-bit EXEC mask; a uniform branch (split at 0 or at the full width) skips the untaken phase entirely, which is the "cycles if every lane agreed" reading above.
