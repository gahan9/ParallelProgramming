Cheapest and least risky fix first

Four levers, applied in the module's stated order: stop calling the model at all, serve it better, shrink the numbers, shrink the model. Each is more invasive and more risky than the last — slide them and watch how rarely you need to reach the expensive end.

1· Cache / cascade hit-rate (lowest risk): 30%
2· Serve better — batching / runtime / graph capture: 2.0×
3· Shrink the numbers — quantization: 2.0×
4· Shrink the model — distillation (highest risk): 1.0×
Target cost budget: 25% of baseline
