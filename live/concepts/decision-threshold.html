The threshold is a cost ratio, not a knob

A classifier outputs a score, not a decision. The threshold that turns one into the other comes from what a false positive costs against what a false negative costs — slide either one and watch the cheapest threshold move off 0.5.

The principle is the citable part — C01 section 2: define the decision before the prediction, and set the threshold from the relative cost of the two error types, not from a metric's default.
