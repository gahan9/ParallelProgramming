Peak and bandwidth here are illustrative, not any vendor's spec. They exist to let the roof itself move so you can see the ridge point shift. Published ceilings are a starting point, not an answer — B02's lab derives real peak FLOP/s and bandwidth from your own hardware, and every kernel this curriculum writes plots left of the ridge on real silicon (B02 section 5), which is the argument for fusion in one picture.
