Reference

The lookup surface. When you are mid-lab and cannot remember whether a wavefront is 32 or 64, or what the ridge point actually divides, this answers it without making you re-read the module.

Every fact carries a link to the module section or glossary entry that derives it. Nothing here is restated from memory, and where a number appears it is the module's number. If a sheet and its source disagree, the source wins. Testing yourself is a different job — that is the recall cards.

Four tiers, four questions. A track sheet answers "what does this part of the curriculum own, and what do I need before starting it". A concept sheet answers "what do I need to hold in my head about memory, or collectives, or precision" — it crosses modules, because the concepts do. A module card answers "what was the one idea in A04 again". An analogy card answers "what is this actually like", and then immediately tells you where that picture stops being true — which is the half people skip and the half the engineering lives in.

Print it. Ctrl-P gives one concept sheet per landscape page with the citation URLs spelled out, so the printout stands on its own away from the browser.

Facts live in data/reference.json, not in this file, so they have one home and can be checked mechanically. Pinned sheets are remembered in localStorage under ppc.sheets.v1; nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
